TradePilot 1.1.0 adds **XAUUSD gold scalping on MetaTrader 5**. It is the new home screen. Everything from 1.0.0, including Delta Exchange, works as before.

> **Please test on a demo account first.** The MetaTrader 5 integration has been tested with automated tests and a simulated terminal, but has not yet been run against a real MetaTrader 5 terminal or broker by the developers. Use a demo account until you have verified it yourself (see *Demo testing* in the documentation). No strategy guarantees profits.

## What's new

- **Gold Scalper.** One page with:
  - the live XAUUSD price and spread;
  - the M15 and M1/M5 trend and the current BUY/SELL signal, with entry, stop-loss and take-profit;
  - ATR and volatility;
  - your open trades, today's profit/loss, win rate and win/loss streaks;
  - your account balance and equity, plus the MetaTrader 5 connection and bot status.
- **Controls:** Start/Stop Scalping, Auto Trading ON/OFF, Manual BUY/SELL, close one trade or all, change stop-loss/take-profit, and Emergency Stop.
- **Your broker's gold symbol is found automatically:** XAUUSD, XAUUSDm, GOLD and other variations. Lot sizes follow your broker's own contract size, lot step and minimum stop distance.
- **Scalping strategy, editable on the page:**
  - M15 trend with M1 or M5 entries;
  - EMA 9/20/50, RSI, MACD and ATR, plus candle confirmation;
  - optional VWAP;
  - pullback, breakout or momentum entries.

  A trade needs several confirmations; one indicator alone never opens a trade.
- **Risk controls** with conservative defaults:
  - risk per trade or fixed lots;
  - maximum open trades, daily loss, trades per day and losses in a row;
  - maximum spread and minimum reward : risk;
  - cooldown after a trade and maximum trade duration;
  - trading session hours;
  - filters against chasing extended moves and against sudden spikes;
  - high-risk event windows, which you enter yourself (there is no news feed).
- **Demo and Live modes.** Demo mode only trades a demo account, and Live mode only a real-money account. A running bot stops sending orders if the terminal switches to a different account. Orders on your account need **Auto Trading** turned on with your password; a real-money account also needs its account number.
- **Gold backtests** on your broker's M1/M5 candles, with real spreads, commission per lot and slippage:
  - net profit, win rate, profit factor and maximum drawdown;
  - average trade duration and average win and loss;
  - number of trades.
- **MetaTrader 5 next to Delta Exchange.** Choose the active broker in Settings → General.
- **Strategy builder.** New M1 and M30 timeframes, MACD and VWAP indicators, multi-timeframe indicators, and entry filters.

## Getting started with gold (Windows)

1. Install the MetaTrader 5 terminal from your broker. Open a **demo** account, log in, and turn on **Algo Trading**.
2. In TradePilot, go to **Settings → MetaTrader 5**: enter the server, login and password, then choose **Test connection**.
3. In **Trading → Account risk settings**, raise *Max order size* to about 5,000. The default blocks even 0.01 lot of gold, which is about one ounce.
4. Open **Gold Scalper**. Start in paper mode, with Auto Trading off, and run a backtest. Only then turn on Auto Trading on the demo account.

Check with your broker that it accepts clients from your country.

## Upgrading

Windows and Linux update automatically from 1.0.0. Your password, strategies, settings and history are kept, and the database is backed up before it is upgraded. macOS: download the new `.dmg`.

## Known limitations

- **Not yet verified on a real terminal.** MetaTrader 5 has not been tested against a real terminal or broker by the developers. Use a demo account first.
- **MetaTrader 5 needs Windows.** On Linux and macOS the Gold Scalper shows "Windows only".
- **No economic-news feed.** Enter scheduled releases as high-risk events.
- **Simplified market data.** VWAP uses MetaTrader 5 tick volume, and backtests use candles (M1 at the finest), not ticks.
- **Subscription server not connected.** As in 1.0.0, the subscription server is not configured in this build, so features that need a subscription stay locked until a later release.
- **Unsigned installers.** Windows shows a SmartScreen prompt (**More info → Run anyway**).

Trading gold and derivatives carries a high risk of loss. TradePilot is a tool, not financial advice.
